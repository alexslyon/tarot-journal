# Claude's Reference Descriptions

The editorial blurbs written for the Reference sections when they
were first built (2026-08-28). Removed from the UI in favor of
per-entity source texts; kept here so they can be pasted back in
under a "Claude's Description" reference source if wanted. The
same texts also remain in reference_content.py.

## Signs

- **Aries** — Initiative, courage, self-assertion, the spark that starts things.
- **Taurus** — Stability, embodiment, patience, material pleasure and worth.
- **Gemini** — Curiosity, exchange, duality, language and quick connection.
- **Cancer** — Nurture, memory, home, protective feeling and belonging.
- **Leo** — Radiance, creative self-expression, generosity, sovereignty.
- **Virgo** — Discernment, craft, service, refinement of the useful.
- **Libra** — Balance, relationship, aesthetics, weighing and fairness.
- **Scorpio** — Intensity, transformation, the hidden, death-and-rebirth.
- **Sagittarius** — Aspiration, the long view, philosophy, the aimed arrow.
- **Capricorn** — Ambition, structure, mastery through time and limitation.
- **Aquarius** — The collective, ideals, invention, detachment and reform.
- **Pisces** — Dissolution, compassion, dream and imagination, the boundless.

## Planets

- **Sun** — Vitality, identity, purpose, the conscious center.
- **Moon** — Instinct, rhythm, memory, the reflective and receptive.
- **Mercury** — Mind, language, exchange, travel between realms.
- **Venus** — Attraction, harmony, value, art and affection.
- **Mars** — Drive, courage, conflict, the cutting edge of will.
- **Jupiter** — Expansion, fortune, meaning, generosity and excess.
- **Saturn** — Limitation, time, discipline, the structures that endure.
- **Uranus** — Rupture, awakening, invention, the sudden and original.
- **Neptune** — Dissolution, glamour, the mystical and oceanic.
- **Pluto** — The underworld, compulsion, destruction and regeneration.

## Sephiroth

- **Kether** (Crown) — The undifferentiated source; unity before any division.
- **Chokmah** (Wisdom) — Pure dynamic force; the first flash of creative energy.
- **Binah** (Understanding) — Form and limitation; the womb that gives force a shape.
- **Chesed** (Mercy) — Expansive, ordering benevolence; building and abundance.
- **Geburah** (Severity) — Restriction and rigor; the strength that cuts away.
- **Tiphareth** (Beauty) — The reconciling center; harmony, sacrifice, the heart.
- **Netzach** (Victory) — Desire, instinct, and feeling; the energies of nature.
- **Hod** (Splendour) — Intellect and form-giving thought; language and magic.
- **Yesod** (Foundation) — The astral matrix; image, dream, and the machinery of change.
- **Malkuth** (Kingdom) — The manifest world; everything brought into body and matter.

## Chakras

- **Root** (Muladhara) — Survival, grounding, safety, belonging to the body and the earth.
- **Sacral** (Svadhisthana) — Pleasure, desire, creativity, emotional flow.
- **Solar Plexus** (Manipura) — Will, confidence, personal power, digestion of experience.
- **Heart** (Anahata) — Love, compassion, connection, the bridge between lower and upper.
- **Throat** (Vishuddha) — Expression, truth-telling, voice, listening.
- **Third Eye** (Ajna) — Insight, imagination, inner vision, discernment beyond the senses.
- **Crown** (Sahasrara) — Unity, transcendence, connection to what is larger than the self.

## Numbers

- **0 · The Void / Infinite Potential** — Nothingness and everything. The unmanifest, pure potential before form. Freedom from limitation. *The Fool — the leap into the unknown, beginningless beginning.*
- **1 · Unity / Beginnings** — Individuality, initiative, will, leadership, new starts. The seed, the self, the singular point of origin. *The Magician and Aces — raw potential channeled into action.*
- **2 · Duality / Balance** — Partnership, polarity, receptivity, patience, choice between opposites. The first division — self and other. *The High Priestess and Twos — the threshold between known and unknown.*
- **3 · Creation / Expression** — Growth, creativity, synthesis, expansion, abundance. What emerges from the union of two — the child, the fruit. *The Empress and Threes — creative abundance and early fruition.*
- **4 · Structure / Foundation** — Stability, order, discipline, foundation, hard work. The four walls, the four directions — manifestation made solid. *The Emperor and Fours — authority, structure, and groundedness.*
- **5 · Change / Conflict** — Disruption, freedom, adventure, instability, challenge. The number that breaks the settled four — crisis and growth. *The Hierophant and Fives — upheaval that leads to deeper understanding.*
- **6 · Harmony / Responsibility** — Love, beauty, balance, nurturing, duty, home. Equilibrium restored after the disruption of five. *The Lovers and Sixes — choices made from the heart, reciprocity.*
- **7 · Introspection / Mystery** — Wisdom, analysis, solitude, spirituality, inner work. The seeker turning inward — not everything is visible. *The Chariot and Sevens — inner mastery and faith through uncertainty.*
- **8 · Power / Mastery** — Achievement, abundance, karma, material success, regeneration. Infinite energy (the lemniscate) applied with discipline. *Strength and Eights — endurance, control, and the consequences of effort.*
- **9 · Completion / Wisdom** — Fulfillment, humanitarianism, endings, universal compassion. The last single digit — the culmination of a cycle. *The Hermit and Nines — solitary wisdom at the end of a journey.*
- **10 · Cycle / Renewal** — Completion and new beginning combined. The full turn of the wheel — endings that contain seeds of the next cycle. *Wheel of Fortune and Tens — the cycle complete, turning toward what comes next.*
