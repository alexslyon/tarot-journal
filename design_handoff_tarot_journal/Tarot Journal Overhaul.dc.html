Major Arcana XVIII · Water · Pisces

The Moon

{{ k }}

Night knowledge. What is true but not yet sayable — dream, intuition, the thing sensed at the edge of the path. The Moon does not lie; it only refuses daylight terms.

Upright

Trust the reading that arrives without argument. Something is being remembered rather than decided. Fitting for questions about creative work, grief, and anything approached at night.

Reversed

Fog mistaken for depth. Confusion dressed as mystery, or a fear held so long it now passes for intuition. Ask what plain fact is being avoided.
